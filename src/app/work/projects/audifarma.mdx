## Project Summary

Audifarma, one of the giants of the healthcare sector, needed far more than a visual refresh — it needed a deep digital transformation. I led the UX/UI restructuring and Front-End integration of an ecosystem spanning three critical fronts: the public portal serving over 10,000 monthly users, a new corporate Intranet, and a B2B services portal. The goal was to unify the experience under a modular design system, restoring operational autonomy to the company and ensuring long-term scalability.

## The Challenge: Modernizing a Legacy Architecture

The platform's starting point presented critical architecture and UX issues that made daily operations unsustainable:

- **Hardcoded Code, Zero Modularity:** The platform had no component system. All content was hardcoded, turning every update into a bottleneck.
- **Outdated, Rigid Design:** An interface that didn't meet modern visual accessibility standards.
- **No Responsive Design:** The site didn't adapt to mobile devices, locking out thousands of users trying to manage their healthcare services from their phones.

![Audifarma Before - Outdated Design](/images/projects/audifarma/audifarma-web-02.jpg)
<small>**Starting point (before the redesign):** Static, non-responsive design with a hardcoded architecture that limited the ecosystem's scalability.</small>

## Architecture of the New Ecosystem

To eliminate this technical debt, I designed and implemented specific solutions for each business stakeholder:

- **Corporate Intranet (for 6,000+ Employees):** Designed an internal portal from scratch centered on communication and talent retention. It includes a dynamic news system, newsletters, circulars, and a corporate document center — radically streamlining HR workflows.
- **Pharmacological Research Hub:** UX/UI development of a specialized blog with 200+ medical articles online. Designed an advanced search and filtering system (by date, category, title, and content) to make dense, technical information accessible.
- **B2B Portal (Reactive Tooling):** For the supply chain division, designed and built highly reactive data-query interfaces for suppliers, improving the speed of logistics management.
- **Autonomy on dotCMS:** The entire public ecosystem was built with a modular approach, integrating the new responsive components directly into dotCMS so the communications team could manage the portal without depending on IT.

## Technologies Used

- **Figma:** Built the unified Design System for the Web, Intranet, and Supplier Portal.
- **dotCMS:** Template integration and visual backend structuring.
- **Reactive Front-End Logic:** Built dynamic interfaces for supplier tools and the research blog's search engine.

## Result & Scale of Impact

Delivered a robust, 100% responsive digital ecosystem. The 6,000 employees now have a centralized communication tool, suppliers get real-time data through reactive interfaces, and the historical friction between the design team and IT disappeared thanks to a component-based system.
